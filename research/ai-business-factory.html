Project overview

AI Business Factory

AI Business Factory is a separate project focused on turning business objectives into verifiable software-supported workflows. It does not rely on Cognitive OS and should be able to operate as an independent product.

Research and product direction

The work emphasizes clear requirements, persistent task state, recoverable execution, spending boundaries and artifact checks. A plausible AI-generated business plan alone is not a completed business.

Current, verifiable stage

A local prototype has been implemented and its repository documents automated tests. It can produce and validate a local offer website and accept local inquiries. A publicly deployed product, payments, customer management and a complete autonomous business-operation cycle are not yet implemented.

Relevant collaboration

Controlled pilot infrastructure, secure integration engineering, practical business-workflow expertise and eventual early testers.
