Project overview

Child & Family Ecosystem

Child & Family Ecosystem is a long-term educational vision combining interactive worlds, learning experiences, stories, exploration and creativity across languages and cultures.

Research and product direction

The intended scope includes reading, writing, mathematics, languages, science, virtual laboratories, creative activities and future subjects or modes of learning that cannot yet be anticipated. No particular age group, subject list or technical format defines a permanent limit on the vision.

Current, verifiable stage

This is currently a concept under development, not an operating international learning platform. Learning outcomes, adoption and completed production features are not claimed.

Relevant collaboration

Educational researchers, multilingual learning partners, accessibility expertise, child-appropriate design and infrastructure collaboration.
