Project overview

Cognitive OS / AION

Cognitive OS / AION is an experimental effort to explore a single persistent artificial being that could gain capabilities, learn from experience, investigate unfamiliar questions, recover from failures and develop its own mechanisms for continued improvement. This is not intended to be a conventional chatbot, an API wrapper or a collection of independent agents.

Research and product direction

Research themes include persistent memory, parallel exploratory worlds, verification of inherited learning, self-repair and continual improvement of learning and evaluation mechanisms. The long-term vision is deliberately open-ended.

Current, verifiable stage

The private research repository contains experimental modules, laboratory mechanisms and test contracts. They are engineering work, not independent evidence of an intelligence breakthrough. AGI, ASI and sustained autonomous intelligence growth have not been demonstrated.

Relevant collaboration

Technical review of evaluation methods, reproducible experiments, secure research infrastructure and computing resources.
